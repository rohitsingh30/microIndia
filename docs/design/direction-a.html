Ledger

An analyst's desk, not a social app. The brand marketer and the agency planner are making a decision they will have to defend, so the screen behaves like a well-kept research ledger: hairline rules instead of floating cards, tabular figures set in a monospace so numbers line up and read as measured, and one colour (peacock, a deep teal with Indian roots that isn't the default SaaS blue or purple) reserved for action and selection. Density is high but calm: 13px base, a strict 4px grid, 2px corners, no shadows, no gradients outside reel keyframes. Fit is shown by a monochrome glyph plus a word, never by colour or a score. AI-written text sits at 15px in the same family, so it reads as the answer while the evidence sits beside it in smaller mono.
