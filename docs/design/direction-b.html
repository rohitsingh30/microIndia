Casebook

Each creator is a case the marketer has to make to someone else, so the page reads like a well-edited brief: evidence first, then the argument. Reel keyframes lead every card at a size you can actually judge. Two typefaces split the job: Schibsted Grotesk, a newspaper grotesk, carries every number, label and control; Newsreader, a text serif, is used only for what the AI wrote (why she fits, the summary, hooks, pitch angles), so a reader always knows what was measured and what is our reading of it. Colour is almost absent. Primary actions are plain ink, and a single marigold "highlighter" marks the evidence that backs a claim: the 4.3× on a reel, your place on the peer bar, the active tab. More air than Ledger: a 15px base, 24–32px gaps and one column at 1200px.

Notes on this screen

The work leads. Each card opens on a large portfolio tile of this kind of cake, because that's what the mother is judging first.

The highlighter marks the words that made it a match ("theme cakes for kids", "next to Kilpauk"), the same way it marks evidence in the brand app.

No stars, no "verified", no "replies fast". Unknowns are written as what to do next ("Ask if eggless", "Prices not posted. Ask them.").

Instagram DM is always available, so it's always the primary button. WhatsApp only appears when the business shows it publicly.
